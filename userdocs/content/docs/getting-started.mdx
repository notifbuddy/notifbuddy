## Steps

1. **Connect Slack** — authorize the workspace so notifbuddy can post.
2. **Connect Linear** — install the app to sync issues and statuses.
3. **Configure channel rules** — pick when channels open (see [Templates](/templates)).

Prefer the terminal? The [CLI](/cli) covers the same steps with
`notifbuddy connect` and `notifbuddy settings`, and its bundled skill lets a
coding agent such as Claude Code run the whole onboarding for you.

## Invite your team

Invite teammates from **Settings → Organization**, either by typing an email
address or with **Import from Slack**, which lists your synced Slack workspace
members so you can select several at once. Each selected person still receives
an email invitation. The picker only lists people who can actually be invited:
members whose email isn't visible to the Slack app, and people who are already
members or already invited, are left out (a footnote shows how many). If
someone is missing, use the re-sync button in the picker to refresh the member
list from Slack.
